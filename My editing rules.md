# My editing rules

Claude reads this before every edit and follows it over its own defaults.
When you tell Claude to do something differently, it adds it here.
Hook in the first 2 seconds. Start mid-action or on the line. No “hey guys”, no walking into frame, no intro.
Cut dead air. Pauses go. The only pause that stays is the deadpan one before or after a punchline.
Keep the mess. The kid interrupting, the Dexcom alarm, the dropped spoon all stay if they’re true or funny. Cut boring, not real.
Jump cuts only. No transitions, no zoom effects, no stickers, no filters, no beauty mode. Fix exposure only if it’s unwatchable.
Length. Cooking: 30 to 45 seconds. Talk-to-camera: up to 90 if it holds.
On-screen captions. Burned in, sentence case, 3 to 6 words per chunk, your exact words. No emojis, no em dashes. Swearing stays in the audio.
Text stays in the safe zone. Nothing in the bottom fifth or down the right side, where the app buttons sit.
No “follow for more”. End on the reaction, the result, or a question.
Kids-Miss 5 / Miss 3 only. No school uniforms, school names or anything that shows where you live.
Cooking is a fast montage. Slow down for: something going wrong, a kid interrupting or helping, something you say out loud.
